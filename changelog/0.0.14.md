- Cancel captured pointer interactions on Escape, a fresh press after a missed release, and removal or replacement of the active subtree.
- Keep left-button drags active when another mouse button is released. Always release capture after a drop handler completes or throws.

- Cover preview cleanup, live row removal, root replacement, runtime closure, cancellation, lost release recovery and throwing drop handlers on all three modern runtimes. The generated Forge 1.16.5 port inherits the same fixes.
- Make ScrollView scrollbar thumbs and tracks interactive. Preserve the grab position, capture drags outside the viewport, and cancel on Escape, detachment or disappearing overflow.
